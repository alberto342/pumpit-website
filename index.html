Real Android screens. A number in a screenshot is one example, not a result PumpIt promises.

Every plan

Know today's energy before you start.

The score is today's training energy, from 0 to 100. It is a training signal, not a medical diagnosis. The 83 on this card is one example.

Morning readiness comes from sleep, recovery, and recent training. Activity during the day, time awake, and a limited nap credit can move the current score. PUMPIT STRESS is shown beside it and does not change the score.

  • Plus can offer a lighter session next to the original
  • AI Pro can add a workout for today only
  • A low score never blocks Start Workout

Every plan

Swap the exercise without ending the session.

From the live workout, open Replace Exercise and pick a reason: no equipment, equipment busy, too difficult, or pain or discomfort.

Free is a manual swap. Plus suggests options for the same muscles. AI Pro ranks them as AI Recommended. The swap is for this session. Pain or discomfort is a reason to change the exercise, not a medical assessment.

  • Stay on the same workout screen
  • Manual choice, a suggestion, or an AI ranking
  • Log the exercise you actually did

Plus · AI Pro

Shorten today when time runs out.

Choose how long you have, from 15 to 40 minutes, and start a shorter version of today.

Higher-priority lifts stay. Lower-priority accessories go first, and sets come down on the work in between. The saved weekly program is not rewritten.

  • Choose a time, then start
  • Keep the lifts that matter most
  • The regular plan stays as it is

Plus

See if the week is light, in range, or heavy.

Training Load adds up the PumpIt sessions you logged, from duration and intensity, with volume and heart rate when those were logged too.

The detail marks the week Low, Optimal, or High against a range, and the chart runs from 7 days to a year. At first the range follows your experience and training days. After enough weeks it is labeled your optimal range. It is a training view, not a medical measure.

  • Built from the sessions you logged
  • Low, Optimal, or High against a range
  • Your own range once there is enough history

AI Pro

See when the week moved off your baseline.

AI Training Monitor compares training load, volume, strength, energy, and consistency with your recent baseline.

It flags a change when something meaningful moved, and explains that change in plain language. The 68% on this screen is one example, not a typical week and not a medical finding.

  • One place for what changed this week
  • Load, volume, strength, energy, consistency
  • A note only when the shift is large enough

AI Coach · AI Pro

Understand what's improving and what to focus on next.

Coach Score is a weekly number from consistency, strength, workout quality, and recovery. The coach can explain it. It does not invent the number.

The four parts show what is holding the week back. You choose the next sessions.

  • Consistency, strength, quality, recovery
  • A note on what moved
  • You choose the next workout

AI Pro

A plan change only if you apply it.

Adaptive Suggestion proposes a specific change when training load is high, energy is falling, or a skip pattern is strong enough.

That change can be a swap, an earlier placement, or less volume. Nothing is written to the program until you tap Apply Change. Keep Current Plan leaves it as it is. There is no background job editing the plan.

  • A specific change, not a vague tip
  • Apply Change, or Keep Current Plan
  • The program stays yours until you confirm

Questions

Before you download or subscribe.

What is included for free?

The Free plan covers core training: log sets, reps, and weight, build a plan by hand, swap an exercise yourself, and keep recent history and records. You do not need a trial to start. Free, Plus, and AI Pro are all ad-free.

What is the difference between Plus and AI Pro?

Plus adds the full program library, a program matched to your profile, suggested exercise replacements, Short on Time, and strength and month-to-month progress. AI Pro includes Plus, and adds the coach: a plan built in chat, ranked replacements, Adaptive Suggestion, Coach Score, Training Monitor, and nutrition.

Do I have to use AI?

No. Free and Plus work without the coach. AI Pro is optional.

Does PumpIt support a watch right now?

Not yet. PUMPIT Watch, heart rate, workout controls on the watch, and advanced Energy on the watch are marked Coming Soon. They are not available in the current Android app.

How do I manage a subscription?

Google Play shows the price and currency before you purchase, and bills Plus and AI Pro. Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play. Deleting a PumpIt account does not cancel the Play subscription. Help and account deletion are on the support page.
