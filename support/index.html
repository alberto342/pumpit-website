Support

PumpIt support is handled in the app, after you sign in. That keeps the request with your account, and you can attach a screenshot. This website does not store messages.

Send a request

  1. Open PumpIt on Android and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Support.
  3. Choose a topic, describe what happened, and add a screenshot if the screen is part of the problem.

What to include

  • What you were trying to do
  • What happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • Any error message you saw
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Topics the app already covers

The in-app form groups requests so they reach the right place.

Workouts and programs

Starting a session, edits that did not save, Find, or Build Plan.

Progress

Weight, progress photos, overall progress, or a missing personal record.

Account and billing

Sign-in, plan features, restore purchases, or payments through Google Play.

AI features

AI Coach, reviews, nutrition, or a plan suggestion. These require AI Pro.

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Deleting your PumpIt account does not cancel a Plus or AI Pro subscription. Steps for account removal are on the delete account page. Common questions are on the FAQ.
